## Summary

Fixes a reconnect loop seen against a wayvnc host with a single-client guard. Closing a session
window with its X button previously left its tab's retry task running. A newly opened window then
created a second VNC connection, and the server's guard disconnected the older one on each new
connection. Both invisible and visible sessions could keep reconnecting and displacing each other.

Window close now cancels the tab's pending and in-flight connections, releases its RFB transport,
and ignores late callbacks. A connection that is dropped immediately after a successful handshake
also counts as a failed reconnect; three such attempts close the tab rather than resetting the
counter and retrying forever.
